## Format and eligibility

- In-person, 24-hour team hackathon. **Teams of 2–4.** We are 4.
- Open to university students at institutions in Singapore, any discipline.
- Every member must have completed individual LifeHack registration.
- One team per participant for the Main Hackathon.

## Project rules (from the official rules page)

- Must address one of the **official challenge statements**.
- Development must take place **primarily during the hackathon period**.
- Open-source libraries, frameworks, APIs, datasets, starter templates and existing dev tools are
  **allowed**.
- **Any significant pre-existing code, assets, datasets or templates must be clearly disclosed.**
- Submitting an existing completed project as new is **not permitted**.
- Must be the team's own work; no plagiarism.
- Must have permission to use all code, data and media included.
- Any technology stack is fine unless a specific challenge statement says otherwise.
- Policies on generative AI, sponsor APIs, IP and confidential data were listed as "to be
  clarified before the event" — **confirm at the briefing**.

## Submission requirements

- Submit before the deadline. **One Devpost submission per team.**
- **Every team member must be listed on the submission.**
- Must include: **working prototype + project description + source-code repository +
  demonstration materials**.
- The repo **must contain instructions explaining how to run or evaluate the project**.
- Must be functional enough for judges to evaluate during the **live demonstration**.
- Must clearly acknowledge third-party tools, datasets, APIs, libraries and assets.
- **Late or incomplete submissions may be excluded from judging.**
